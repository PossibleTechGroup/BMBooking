# Patient Capabilities - BM Booking Telegram Bot

This document lists everything a patient can do in the BM Booking ecosystem.
The Telegram Mini App onboarding should reflect these features accurately.

---

## 1. Account & Authentication

- Register with a phone number via OTP verification
- Login securely with SMS-based one-time password
- Complete a health profile (name, date of birth, gender, blood type, emergency contact)

## 2. Find Doctors

- Search doctors by name, specialization, or clinic
- Filter by specialty (Cardiology, Neurology, Dermatology, Pediatrics, Orthopedics, Ophthalmology, Gynecology, Internal Medicine, General Surgery)
- Filter by minimum rating (All, 4+, 4.5+, 4.8+)
- Sort by highest rating or nearest distance
- View doctor profiles with bio, experience, consultation fee, clinic, and availability

## 3. Book Appointments

- Book for yourself or for someone else
- Select a medical issue category and describe symptoms
- View recommended documents to bring to the appointment
- Upload referral documents (optional)
- Choose an available date and time slot
- Pay via TeleBirr mobile payment
- Receive booking confirmation

## 4. Manage Appointments

- View all appointments in one place
- Filter by status: Pending, Accepted, Completed, Cancelled
- Cancel a pending or accepted appointment
- Reschedule to a new date and time
- Rate and review a doctor after a completed appointment

## 5. Medical Equipment

- Search medical equipment by name or city
- Filter by category (MRI, CT Scan, Dialysis, Ultrasound, X-Ray, and more)
- View equipment details, hospital info, and operational status
- Book equipment for a specific date and time
- Pay via TeleBirr for equipment bookings
- View and manage lab bookings

## 6. Profile & Settings

- Edit personal information (name, gender, date of birth, blood type, emergency contact)
- Manage notification preferences (reminders, doctor messages, health tips)
- Control privacy settings (medical history sharing, profile access)
- Switch between standard and Ethiopian time/calendar formats
- View privacy policy and terms of service
- Sign out securely

## 7. Hospital Information

- View hospital details, address, and available equipment
- Call hospitals directly
- Get directions via maps

## 8. Announcements

- Browse platform-wide announcements and health updates
